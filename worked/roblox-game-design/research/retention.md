- 1,460 flows from 986 apps; the average app has about 25 onboarding screens.
- The strongest screens sell the outcome, not features. Converting flows optimise for "value, fast," not brevity.
- Self-evident products may need no onboarding at all ("the first prompt is the onboarding"). AI utility apps average 3 screens.
- Personalisation appears in 23% of apps (7% of AI apps). Paywalls appear in 22% of flows, sometimes paired with a quiz and a one-time offer to create urgency.
- Don't copy flows blindly across regions.
- A "+10% relative one-week retention" figure from personalisation is unconfirmed.

## Top techniques for this topic

**1. Fun inside 5 minutes, and kill the first-play bounce.** Backed by ranks 2, 19, 18, 20, 10 and 11.
- First-play bounce rate (<60 s, 61–180 s) is now a negative ranking signal.
- Apply: spawn new players directly into the core loop. Hand out starter items and soft currency on `Players.PlayerAdded`. Defer non-essential systems.
- Watch Creator Hub, Engagement, "New User First Session Retention", plus the bounce charts under Acquisition, Home Recommendations.

**2. Show, don't tell.** Use visual cues, contextual tutorials and timed hints. Backed by ranks 19, 11 and 2.
- Visual cues: use a `Beam` or `ParticleEmitter` trail to the objective, a `Highlight` on interactables, and a `BillboardGui` arrow or a `UIStroke` pulse (via `TweenService`) on the next button.
- Contextual tutorials: fire the tutorial on a first-use event, such as owning two duplicates or entering a zone.
- Timed hints: `task.delay(10, …)`, then show the hint only if the step is still incomplete. Show it once, and store the "seen" flags with `DataStoreService`.

**3. Instrument the funnel and A/B test it.** Backed by ranks 18, 19, 2 and 15.
- Use `AnalyticsService:LogOnboardingFunnelStepEvent(player, step, name)` for FTUE steps. Use `LogFunnelStepEvent` with a GUID `funnelSessionId` for recurring flows such as the shop, and `LogCustomEvent` for anything else.
- Server-only, published games only. Validate client `RemoteEvent` step numbers first.
- Test hint delays, dialogue length and starter currency with Experiments. Read values with `ConfigService:GetConfigForPlayerAsync` and ship the winners through Configs without restarting servers.

**4. Target what Recommended For You actually measures.** Backed by ranks 10, 13, 20, 4, 6 and 3.
- The most important signals are play-through rate (PTR), first-play bounce, play days per user and playtime per user (capped at 60 min per user per day). Each is measured over Day 1, Day 2–7 and Day 8–28.
- The next tier is co-play days, qualified sessions, spend days and Robux per user.
- All signals are per-user averages from players who found the game organically through RFY. Small games are not disadvantaged.
- Apply: compare Home Recommendations signals to benchmark games, fix them in priority order, and expect "explore" spikes after updates.

**5. A distinctive, honest thumbnail and metadata.** Backed by ranks 6, 4, 20 and 3.
- A custom thumbnail is required to get Home impressions.
- Keep 2 or more thumbnails active for thumbnail personalisation. In Roblox's testing this averaged +8.5% qualified play-through rate.
- Match title, images and tags to the gameplay and add a gameplay video. Mismatches cause bounces and reduced exposure. Never lead with "free Robux."

**6. Layered goals and progression for D7.** Backed by ranks 18, 1, 14, 17 and 9.
- Set short, mid and long-term goals: quests, collections, season pass.
- Keep early XP thresholds low, tune the curve live with Configs, and save progress with `DataStoreService`.
- Measure level distribution by logging a funnel step for each level.

**7. Pacing, novelty and moments of joy.** Backed by ranks 1, 9, 14, 17 and 19.
- Introduce a new mechanic or zone regularly; alternate intensity with calm.
- End the FTUE and sessions on a celebration (`TweenService` UI pop, particle burst, sound), then tease the next goal.

**8. Social play and co-play.** Backed by ranks 20, 18, 6 and 15.
- "Intentional co-play days" is a ranking signal.
- Apply: `SocialService:CanSendGameInviteAsync` / `PromptGameInvite`, with `Player:GetJoinData()` launch data to spawn the invitee beside the inviter.
- Add parties, trading and `OrderedDataStore` leaderboards. Reward group play; never require it.

**9. A sustainable live ops cadence driven by players.** Backed by ranks 15 and 18; ranks 5 and 8 are inferred.
- Separate hotfix patches from content updates. Ship small drops on existing systems (pets, maps, quests) every 1–4 weeks and major systems every 2–3 months.
- Announce via Creator Hub, Engagement, Events & Updates (notifies opted-in players), then compare event cohorts in the Retention cohort table.

**10. Respectful re-engagement.** Backed by rank 20, the official docs, and ranks 14 and 1.
- Ask for notification opt-in at a natural moment: `ExperienceNotificationService:CanPromptOptInAsync()` (wrapped in `pcall`), then `PromptOptIn()`.
- Send personalised, actionable messages through the Open Cloud `UserNotification` package.
- Limits: opted-in users aged 13+ only, at most 1 per user per day, and never gate gameplay behind opting in.

**11. Retention first; paid traffic and monetisation second.** Backed by ranks 6, 15 and 3, plus the August 2026 DevForum post.
- Ads seed the retrieval stage, but players acquired through ads are excluded from RFY ranking.
- Roblox's rule of thumb: if retention is low, fix core gameplay. If it is high, add fair, optional monetisation.

## Conflicts and caveats

- **Onboarding length.** Mobbin finds an average of about 25 screens and says brevity matters less than speed to value. Roblox says to keep the FTUE brief, ideally 5 minutes or less. On Roblox, leaving is a single click and bounce is now a ranking signal, so favour short flows. Mobbin's sample is apps with paywalls, not games.
- **Update cadence.** Tizzy ships weekly or bi-weekly. Roblox suggests small updates every 2–4 weeks and big ones every 2–3 months, and explicitly says to prioritise the team's mental health.
- **Stale algorithm advice.** In June 2026 Roblox widened signals from 7 to 28 days, removed qualified play-through rate (QPTR) and added PTR and bounce rate. In August 2026 it added more weight on long-term retention and monetisation. Creator videos made before then, and third-party "rolling 7-day window" claims, are outdated. The "500 highly engaged players" figure is now 250.
- **Ads myths.** Officially, ads neither boost nor penalise RFY ranking. An RFY dip after ads is expected because ad-acquired players are treated as already acquired.
- **Dark patterns to avoid** (the audience is young):
  - Fake urgency or one-time offers like those Mobbin observed.
  - FOMO-heavy limited items, pay-to-win (Tizzy warns against it), and "giveaway" metadata, which Roblox down-ranks.
  - Disguised-ad, bait-and-switch or time-pressure notifications, which Roblox bans.
  - Coercive invite or notification gates.
  - Limited-time events should reward participation, not punish absence.
- **Unverified:** the +10% personalisation figure, revenue figures in titles, "Forge GUI" in an AI summary, and secondary RDC 2026 reports.

## Official Roblox guidance

- **Discovery.** RFY works in two stages: retrieval, then ranking. The doc gives a signal priority table. Signals are per-user averages from organic RFY players only. A custom thumbnail is required, and mismatched, non-unique or giveaway metadata gets reduced exposure. https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery
- **Discovery FAQ.** There is no penalty for a weak start: signals use a moving window and games are re-tested daily. Old games can regrow. Genre is not an explicit ranking factor. Ads don't affect RFY ranking. https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq
- **Retention.** D1 depends on core loop, FTUE and performance. D7 depends on progression. D30 depends on the "ending system": content every 2–4 weeks or 2–3 months, plus social systems. The doc also covers daily and weekly cohort tables. https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention
- **Onboarding and techniques.** Teach the essentials, get to the fun quickly and leave players wanting more. Use visual elements, contextual tutorials and timed hints, tuned with Experiments and Configs. https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding and https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding-techniques
- **Funnel events.** Use `LogOnboardingFunnelStepEvent` and `LogFunnelStepEvent`. Calls are server-only, and up to 10 funnels show on the dashboard. https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/funnel-events
- **Experience notifications.** Opt-in users aged 13+, 1 per user per day, personalised and actionable, no dark patterns, no gating. https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-notifications
- **RFY updates.** The June 2026 change moved to 28-day signals with PTR and bounce rate. The August 2026 post says: "if retention is low, focus on core gameplay first." https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575 and https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-by-building-games-people-want-to-play/4779042
- **LiveOps essentials.** Covers content cadence, major updates, quality-of-life improvements and bug fixes. Listen to players and protect the team's mental health. https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/liveops-essentials
