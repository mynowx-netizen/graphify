## Per-video notes

**1. Mrwhosetheboss (B).** Seen via search snippets of https://twinmind.com/summaries/how-mobile-games-are-designed-to-scam-you-keorx-lni5s and https://chaindesk.ai/tools/youtube-summarizer/how-mobile-games-are-designed-to-scam-you-keoRX-Lni5s, plus https://afkgaming.com/mobileesports/news/youtuber-mrwhosetheboss-faces-backlash-for-calling-pokemon-unite-a-scam
- Uses Pokémon UNITE as the main example of IAP exploiting players. Also covers CoD Mobile and Diablo Immortal.
- Battle-pass-style paid reward tracks push spending, and players end up spending more than they intended.
- Calls the designs "psychologically manipulative" and urges players to "vote with our wallets".
- UNITE players disputed some of his claims.

**2. Vox (A).** Sources: https://mentalfloss.com/article/83600/heres-how-free-phone-apps-make-money and a machine transcript at https://rosetta.to/u/vox/how-free-games-are-designed-to-make-money (2016).
- Items go on sale at many points, and spending is made "as easy as possible". Some play is made frustrating unless you pay.
- Rare "whales" spend hundreds or thousands. Non-payers spend time in a game tuned to the pain points of a susceptible few.
- Gems and gold put distance between the player and real money.
- Pokémon Go is cited as less manipulative.

**3. Going Indie (C).** The description says games "want to empty your wallet more than they want you to have fun." No summary was found.

## Top techniques for this topic

**1. Sell clear value; never sell relief from friction you added.** Backed by: 2 (A), 7 (B), 1 (B), 11, 3, 10 (C).
- Each item should answer "what does it do, why would I want it?" (Roblox's example is the *Doors* flashlight).
- Use Passes (`MarketplaceService:PromptGamePassPurchase`) for permanent perks and Developer Products (`PromptProductPurchase`) for repeatable ones.
- Skip energy timers and paid skips. Roblox says its users dislike them and downvote games that use them.
- Test: is the game fun for someone who never pays?

**2. Design for many small spenders, not a few whales.** Backed by: 7 (B), 2 (A). Rank 12 (B) argues the opposite.
- Offer a range of prices, and a first-purchase deal that does not appear on a player's very first load.
- Track Conversion, ARPPU (average revenue per paying user) and ARPDAU (average revenue per daily user) in Creator Hub > Monetization > Overview. Roblox says high ARPPU with low ARPDAU means a narrow paying base.
- Opt into Managed pricing. Price tests usually need about 60,000 transactions in 30 days.
- Read prices with `GetProductInfoAsync`, never hard-code them, or Roblox Plus discounts and price tests show the wrong price.

**3. Honest merchandising: right item, right time, calm wording.** Backed by: 1 (B), 2 (A), 3, 14, 18 (C).
- Make offers in context, such as a pre-run shop (the *Doors* elevator) or an intermission purchase (*Dress to Impress*). Leave time for the Robux checkout.
- Show one pop-up at a time.
- Keep sale end times on the server (DataStore or Configs) so timers are real and never restart.
- Write "View Item", not "GET IT NOW".
- Show the Robux price on every button so a second currency doesn't hide the real cost.

**4. Treat paid randomness as regulated: disclose it, or replace it.** Backed by: 19 (C), 2 (A), plus binding Roblox policy.
- Check `PolicyService:GetPolicyInfoForPlayerAsync().ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted`.
- Before purchase, show every outcome's % odds, summing to 100%, behind a worded "Details" button (a bare (i) icon is not enough).
- Show luck boosts as numbers, and update the displayed odds live while a boost is active.
- No "nothing" outcome is allowed.
- A bundle that contains a random item counts as random.
- Check `IsPaidItemTradingAllowed` before allowing trades.
- Test AU, BE, NL, UK and BR with Studio's Player Emulator.
- Safest default: randomness earned only through play (no disclosure needed), plus direct sales priced at expected value. Roblox's example: a 10-Robux box with 5% odds becomes a 200-Robux sword.

**5. Run a fair season (battle) pass.** Backed by: 1 (B), 14, 18 (C), plus Roblox's season pass guide.
- Have a free track and a premium track, priced to match its rewards.
- Run about one-month seasons with at least a one-week break.
- Size daily missions to the average session.
- Use a claim button, and auto-grant leftovers at season end.
- Grant past premium tiers to players who upgrade mid-season.
- Give double XP in the final week, and show the time left.
- Start from the Season passes feature package, and save ownership in a DataStore.

**6. Plan faucets and sinks with expected-value math.** Backed by: 6 (B), 7 (B), plus Roblox's economy guide.
- Log every currency source and sink with `AnalyticsService:LogEconomyEvent`.
- Calculate expected value per action. Roblox's fishing example: 70%×10G + 20%×20G + 10%×30G = 14G per cast.
- Give events their own currency and shop.
- Tune live with Experiments and Configs.

**7. Make purchase handling bulletproof.** Backed by: 10 (C), plus Roblox docs.
- Use one server `ProcessReceipt` (or `BindReceiptHandler`).
- Grant the item, save the `PurchaseId` in a DataStore, then return `PurchaseGranted`. On failure, return `NotProcessedYet`. A receipt can run on two servers at once, so check the `PurchaseId` before granting.
- Never grant from `PromptProductPurchaseFinished`.
- For passes, check `UserOwnsGamePassAsync` on `PlayerAdded`.
- Roblox keeps no per-user history, so you must. Products sold outside the game only stay listed while `ProcessReceipt` works.

**8. No pay-to-win against other players.** Backed by: 11, 19 (C), 7 (B), 1 (B).
- In PvP, sell "Expression" items (skins, emotes, stat-less pets). Keep "Enhancement" items for PvE or co-op.
- Roblox says membership perks must not give "a tactical gameplay advantage" or act as a paywall.
- Tourists (players who hop between games) buy for immediate effect. Locals (regulars) buy long-term value.

**9. Build a real game, not a template cash grab.** Backed by: 13, 15, 20, 8, 5 (C), all weak.
- Use original titles and thumbnails. Look-alikes and "free Robux" hints are de-ranked.
- Template passes with no real visits don't count toward DevEx.

**10. Plan around the current payout rules.** Backed by: 4 (A), 9, 16 (C).
- Creators get 70 of every 100 Robux spent in-game.
- Roblox pays for Plus members' 10–20% discount. A Plus sign-up through your game (`PromptRobloxSubscriptionPurchase`) earns 250 Robux a month for 3 months.
- Premium Payouts ended July 24, 2025. Creator Rewards replaced them:
  - 5 Robux a day per Active Spender (someone who spent $9.99+ in the last 60 days) who plays 10+ minutes, if yours is one of their first three games that day.
  - 35% of new or returning users' first $100 over 60 days, if the game averages 100+ daily users.
- DevEx pays $0.0038 per Robux, or $0.0054 for eligible purchases by U.S. players verified as 18+. You need 30,000 Earned Robux and must be 13+.
- Cross-game pass and product sales ended May 30, 2026. Use Robux transfers instead.

## Conflicts and caveats

- **Whales.** Rank 12 courts them; ranks 7 and 2 say that breaks F2P. On Roblox, heavy spenders may be children using family money, so don't maximize a few accounts.
- **Battle passes.** Ranks 18 and 14 call them grindy but effective. Roblox's version adds a free track, catch-up XP and retroactive rewards. Its tip to retire the final reward creates fear of missing out (FOMO), so state end dates clearly.
- **Limited time vs. pressure.** Roblox endorses limited items and trading, but bans always-on "sales", restarting timers, false "almost gone" claims and pushy wording to minors.
- **Dark patterns to avoid:**
  - entry paywalls, or Premium/Plus required to play
  - timers you then sell skips for
  - hidden odds, "nothing" outcomes, unlabelled (i) icons
  - currency layers that hide the Robux cost
  - pay-to-win in PvP
  - passes that underdeliver
  - stacked pop-ups, and buy prompts while the player can be hurt
  - implying free Robux
- **Outdated advice.** Premium Payouts ended in 2025. DevEx rose from $0.0035 to $0.0038 on Sept 5, 2025, and the minimum is now 30,000 (People Make Games' 100K/$350 is from 2021). "Game passes" are now "Passes". Cross-game sales ended in 2026.
- **Unverified.** All Robux-earning claims (ranks 5, 8, 9, 15, 20). Mrwhosetheboss's UNITE claims were disputed. People Make Games' revenue-share figures vary by outlet.
- **Coverage gap.** 14 videos are C, with techniques inferred from titles only.

## Official Roblox guidance

- **Presentation.** Genuine discounts only. No false scarcity and no restarting timers. Avoid "GET IT NOW" wording with minors. Use PolicyService for gated features. https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization
- **Paid random items.** Odds that sum to 100% before purchase, luck boosts disclosed, alternatives for restricted users, a separate trading check, and no zero-value outcomes. Regulated markets: AU, BE, NL, UK, BR. https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items and https://devforum.roblox.com/t/clarifying-requirements-for-paid-random-items/4654622
- **Purchase handling.** ProcessReceipt guarantees, retries and duplicate-server caveats; store history yourself. https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products and https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/reference/engine/classes/MarketplaceService.md
- **Design guides.** Shop, contextual purchases, season passes, economies. https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/monetization-foundations, https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design, https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/balance-virtual-economies
- **Metrics and pricing.** https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization and https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/managed-pricing
- **Payouts.** https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-rewards (replaced https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/engagement-based-payouts), https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/roblox-plus, https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange
- **Discovery.** Look-alike metadata and implied free-Robux games are de-ranked. https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improving-user-experience-by-reducing-exposure-of-low-quality-content/3299107
